## Task 1: ideate new topics beyond the current framework (ideation only — NO changes)

User asked, based on `research-topics.csv`, to "think of new topics that don't
necessarily have to fit in the current framework." Treated as pure ideation
(like the 2026-06-11 session): nothing in the CSV / parse.mjs / data.js /
index.html was changed. Only PROMPT.md and this memory file were touched.

### Corpus characterization (the basis for the gap analysis)
Read all 192 nodes via `csvToRecords(fs.readFileSync('research-topics.csv'))`
(note: that fn takes the raw TEXT, not pre-parsed rows). Distribution:
v5=56, v6=35, v7=71, v8=30. Four implicit **node types**: fields (v5/v6/v8),
creators (v7 Group A), seminal books (v7 Group B), crafts (v7 Group C /
v8 Tier C). Ten disciplines = the v5 canonical study phases.

**Center of gravity:** systems/cybernetics/complexity, formal structure
(category/type/topology), cognition, computation, design & craft, biology of
*form*. **Negative space (blind spots):** power/conflict, the body/medicine,
money-as-institution, the sacred/ritual, the physical substrate of
civilization (energy/infrastructure), chemistry, and adversarial thinking.

### Ideas delivered (three registers)
1. **Register A — discipline-shaped gaps that fit but are absent:** Chemistry &
   Reaction Networks, Cryptography & Coding Theory, Sociology & Social Network
   Analysis, STS / Actor-Network Theory (Latour), Military Strategy & Theory of
   War, Ritual/Liturgy/Religious Studies, Geology & Plate Tectonics, Accounting
   & Double-Entry Bookkeeping, Energy Systems (Vaclav Smil), Translation
   Studies, Microbiology & the Microbiome, Thanatology.
2. **Register B — NEW node types the framework can't represent (the
   "doesn't fit" core):** (1) cross-cutting concept/lens nodes — Emergence,
   Scaling Laws & Allometry (Geoffrey West), Recursion, Criticality, Modularity;
   (2) tools/instruments/notations that changed thought — Clock, Map, Index,
   Spreadsheet, Printing Press; (3) biographies of materials — Glass, Paper,
   Concrete, Silicon, Salt; (4) grand problems as nodes — Hard Problem of
   Consciousness, Measurement Problem, Protein Folding. Noted these would need a
   new `node_type` column but the pipeline is version-agnostic enough to absorb
   a `v9` cleanly.
3. **Register C — people/books the corpus's own taste implies but skipped.**
   Key pattern: **many existing topics have eponymous founders who aren't
   creator-nodes** — Information Theory but no Shannon; Cybernetics but no
   Wiener; Semiotics + Pragmatism but no Peirce; Enactivism but no Varela;
   Pattern Languages references Christopher Alexander yet he's only a
   `connects_to` string, never a node (same for Buckminster Fuller). Also
   flagged the diversity / non-Western gap as a source of genuinely new
   material: Panini (formal generative grammar), Ramon Llull, I Ching as
   combinatorial system, Needham's Chinese science, Ubuntu, Navya-Nyaya.
   Implied books: Hesse's *The Glass Bead Game* (the novel that IS this
   project), Alexander's *A Pattern Language*, McLuhan's *Understanding Media*,
   Lakoff & Johnson's *Metaphors We Live By*, McCloud's *Understanding Comics*.
