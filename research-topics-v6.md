## Navigation

Use this file alongside `External/Misc/research-topics-v5.md`. v5 remains the canonical 56-topic roadmap; v6 is a *supplement* that identifies the most valuable topics one would add to it.

The new topics are grouped into the same tier language v5 uses:

- **Tier 1 — Core Bridges** → **Tier A** additions (maximum cross-domain leverage)
- **Tier 2 / 3 — Foundations, Design, Representation** → **Tier B** additions (strong specialists)
- **Tier 4 — Specialized Deep-Dives & Niche Synthesis** → **Tier C** additions (narrower but distinctive)

## Method

Every candidate was checked against two exclusion lists:

1. The 38 topic folders currently indexed in `Knowledge/index.md`.
2. The 56 topics canonicalized in `research-topics-v5.md` (including both the tier tables and the master table at the bottom).

A candidate was kept only if it cleared both lists *and* bridged at least two vault topics. Candidates that substantially overlap with topics already in v5 (e.g. "Behavioral Economics" vs Decision Theory & Rationality) were excluded — see **Deliberate Omissions** below.

## Tier A — High-Leverage Bridges

Eight topics that each connect to five or more existing vault topics. These are the highest-priority candidates if v5 is ever expanded into a "v6 canonical" set.

### Fractal Geometry & Self-Similarity
*Mandelbrot, Hausdorff, Barnsley, Falconer, Peitgen*
Self-similar structure across scales — the mathematical skeleton beneath coastlines, lungs, turbulence, market fluctuations, and generative art. Iterated function systems, fractal dimension (Hausdorff, box-counting), L-systems as recursive generators, multifractals, and the diffusion-limited-aggregation algorithms that show up in both biology and creative code. Oddly absent from v5 despite Wolfram's rule-space, chaos theory, morphogenesis, and algorithmic art all implicitly depending on it. A natural bridge because fractality is the *form* that nonlinear dynamics takes and the *aesthetic* that generative art inherits.

**Connects to:** Complex Systems & Chaos Theory, Geometry & Topology, Morphogenesis, Algorithmic Art & Creative Coding, Stephen Wolfram, Biomimicry, Dynamical Systems

### Enactivism & Enactive Cognition
*Varela, Maturana, Thompson, Rosch, Di Paolo, Gallagher*
The "fourth E" of 4E cognition (embodied, embedded, extended, enacted) — the view that cognition is a *self-constituting activity* of the living system, not a representational layer laid over matter. Autopoiesis, sense-making, participatory sense-making, and the radical claim that the mind has no pre-given world to represent because the world is co-enacted. Sits between the phenomenology v5 plans and the ecological/affordance line, and is the conceptual bridge that ties Varela's work in Artificial Life to his work in cognition.

**Connects to:** Cognitive Science, Philosophy of Mind, Artificial Life, Ecological Psychology, Distributed Cognition, Eastern Philosophy, Robotics & Embodied Intelligence

### Process Philosophy
*Whitehead, Bergson, Deleuze, Simondon, Rescher*
Reality as event, flow, and becoming rather than substance. The metaphysical counterpart to dynamical systems and morphogenesis: Simondon's *individuation* explains how form precipitates out of metastable fields; Whitehead's "actual occasions" furnish a vocabulary for a world modeled by differential equations rather than fixed categories; Bergson's *durée* reframes time as continuous creation rather than discrete sequence. Provides interpretive scaffolding for everything the vault already treats as dynamic, emergent, or self-organizing.

**Connects to:** Dynamical Systems, Morphogenesis, Eastern Philosophy, Phenomenology, Complex Systems, Philosophy of Mind, Cybernetics

### Metaphor Theory & Conceptual Blending
*Lakoff, Johnson, Fauconnier, Turner, Gentner*
How abstract thought is scaffolded by cross-domain mappings — "argument is war," "time is money," "theories are buildings." Conceptual blending (Fauconnier & Turner) is a unified model for analogy, humor, design thinking, and scientific insight. This is both a pragmatic bridge between linguistics, cognitive science, design, and pedagogy, and a *self-referential* topic: it explains why the vault's own project of cross-domain synthesis works cognitively.

**Connects to:** Linguistics, Cognitive Science, Semiotics, Pattern Languages, Rhetoric & Narrative, Writing Systems & Notation, Machine Learning (analogy-based reasoning)

### Memory, Mnemonic Systems & Externalized Knowledge
*Frances Yates, Mary Carruthers, Vannevar Bush, Engelbart, Luria, Ebbinghaus*
The method of loci, memory palaces, medieval mnemonic arts, and the modern continuation of that project in Bush's Memex, Engelbart's NLS, Nelson's Xanadu, and today's personal knowledge bases. Uniquely relevant because it directly concerns *the vault's own practice* — why structured external memory amplifies cognition. A tight companion to the planned Writing Systems topic and to Distributed Cognition.

**Connects to:** Distributed Cognition, Writing Systems & Notation, Cognitive Science, History of Computing, Information Retrieval, Information Architecture, HCI

### Ecology & Ecosystem Dynamics
*E. Odum, H.T. Odum, MacArthur, Levins, Holling, Ostrom*
Trophic cascades, energy flows, carrying capacity, resilience, regime shifts, and panarchy — the "systems thinking of living systems." Holling's adaptive cycle is a direct complement to cybernetic feedback and systems dynamics; the Odums' energy-flow accounting is the most concrete expression of thermodynamics applied to biology; Ostrom's commons work provides the institutional bridge to collective intelligence. Essentially the missing ecological layer between Evolution and Systems Thinking.

**Connects to:** Systems Thinking, Cybernetics, Evolution, Collective Intelligence, Economics, Complex Systems, Thermodynamics, Biomimicry

### Type Theory & Homotopy Type Theory
*Martin-Löf, Howard, Voevodsky, Awodey, Coquand*
Propositions-as-types, dependent types, identity types, and the Univalent Foundations programme. The deepest working synthesis of logic, mathematics, and computation — the place where category theory, formal proofs, and programming languages converge into a single subject. A natural successor to Category Theory in the vault and a prerequisite for engagement with proof assistants (Lean, Coq, Agda). Pairs naturally with the v5 topics on Compiler Design and Logic.

**Connects to:** Category Theory, Theory of Computation, Logic & Formal Semantics, Philosophy of Mathematics, Compiler Design, Software Architecture

### Knowledge Graphs, Ontologies & The Semantic Web
*Berners-Lee, Gruber, Guha, Hendler, Horrocks*
RDF, OWL, SPARQL, description logics, and the practical engineering of machine-readable meaning. Directly relevant to the vault's existence as a linked note-base and to the planned NL Querying track. A concrete pairing for the more philosophical Semiotics and Logic tracks, and the backbone of modern retrieval-augmented AI.

**Connects to:** Information Retrieval, NL Querying, Semiotics, Logic & Formal Semantics, AI, NLP, Writing Systems

## Tier B — Strong Specialist Additions

Fifteen topics that cover important domains v5's roadmap currently overlooks. Each bridges three to five existing topics.

### Pragmatism
*Peirce, James, Dewey, Rorty, Brandom*
American pragmatism as a coherent tradition, not just as background for individual figures. Peirce already appears in Semiotics and Dewey in Aesthetics, but the tradition itself — meaning as consequence, truth as what works, inquiry as a social practice — deserves its own treatment. A natural companion to Epistemology and Philosophy of Science, and a bridge to Rorty's late-20th-century revival of pragmatism.

**Connects to:** Semiotics, Aesthetics, Epistemology, Philosophy of Science, Philosophy of Mind, Rhetoric

### Stochastic Processes & Random Walks
*Kolmogorov, Feller, Doob, Itō, Lévy*
Markov chains, Brownian motion, martingales, Poisson processes, stochastic differential equations. The formal layer beneath probability that's silently assumed by DSP, generative music, finance, ML, and agent-based modeling. v5 has Probability but not the *process* side, which is the more practically useful half in any system that evolves in time.

**Connects to:** Probability, DSP, Generative Music, Machine Learning, Dynamical Systems, Economics

### Reinforcement Learning
*Sutton, Barto, Bellman, Silver, Mnih*
Policies, value functions, temporal-difference learning, policy gradients, actor-critic methods, and the decision-theoretic view of AI. The unique topic that bridges decision theory (how action follows from belief), optimal control (Bellman's equations), neuroscience (dopamine and reward prediction error), and modern AI agents (AlphaGo, RLHF). Deserves its own topic separate from the generic ML roadmap item.

**Connects to:** Decision Theory, Machine Learning, Cybernetics, Neuroscience, Robotics, Game Theory, AI Agents

### Probabilistic Programming & Graphical Models
*Pearl, Koller, Goodman, Gelman, Tenenbaum*
Bayesian networks, factor graphs, Markov random fields, and languages like Stan, Pyro, Gen, Church. The working-engineer's synthesis of probability, causal inference, and ML — a natural bridge that makes the abstract probability + causal-inference + ML triangle in v5 actually executable.

**Connects to:** Probability, Causal Inference, Machine Learning, Logic, AI, Cognitive Science (rational analysis)

### Urban Planning & Urbanism
*Jane Jacobs, Kevin Lynch, Jan Gehl, William H. Whyte, Alejandro Aravena*
The city as a complex adaptive system designed at human scale. Jacobs on emergent order in streets, Lynch's *Image of the City* on the cognitive maps of place, Gehl on life between buildings, Whyte's empirical studies of plaza use. A direct extension of Alexander's work into the civic scale and a concrete setting for pattern languages, affordance theory, and collective intelligence.

**Connects to:** Architecture, Christopher Alexander, Pattern Languages, Ecological Psychology, Collective Intelligence, Systems Thinking, Complex Systems

### Astrobiology & Origin of Life
*Oparin, Miller, Szostak, Kauffman, England, Deamer*
Prebiotic chemistry, the RNA world hypothesis, autocatalytic sets, dissipative adaptation, the Drake equation. The scientific question that Artificial Life *theorizes about* and Evolution *starts after*. Kauffman's autocatalytic sets are a direct conceptual cousin to ALife's self-organizing systems; England's dissipative-adaptation work is a live research front linking thermodynamics and origin.

**Connects to:** Artificial Life, Evolution, Complex Systems, Thermodynamics, Morphogenesis, Philosophy of Mind (emergence of agency)

### Functional Programming & Lambda Calculus
*Church, Curry, Hughes, Wadler, Hickey*
A dedicated treatment distinct from Theory of Computation — the lineage from untyped lambda calculus through ML, Haskell, Scala, Clojure, and Rust's borrow system, including practical ideas (persistent data structures, monads, effects, algebraic data types) that currently show up only implicitly in the vault's software topics. Pairs tightly with Category Theory, where many FP concepts have their clean mathematical form.

**Connects to:** Theory of Computation, Category Theory, Type Theory, Compiler Design, Software Architecture, Microservices

### Neuroethology
*Tinbergen, von Frisch, Lorenz, Heiligenberg, Shadmehr*
The neural basis of *natural* behavior in its ecological context — how bats echolocate, how jellyfish swim, how electric fish navigate, how birds learn song. The biological side of what robotics and affordance theory approach computationally and philosophically. Explains *why* Braitenberg's vehicles and Brooks's subsumption architectures work as well as they do.

**Connects to:** Evolution, Cognitive Science, Ecological Psychology, Robotics, Neuroscience, MiroFish, Artificial Life

### Behavioral Ecology & Sociobiology
*Tinbergen, Hamilton, Trivers, E.O. Wilson, Dawkins, West*
Kin selection, reciprocal altruism, parental investment, evolutionarily stable strategies, and the empirical side of evolutionary game theory. The missing biological substrate beneath Game Theory's mathematical treatment of cooperation, and a careful treatment requires engaging the historical controversies around Wilson's sociobiology programme.

**Connects to:** Game Theory, Evolution, Collective Intelligence, Anthropology, Economics, MiroFish

### Complexity Economics & Agent-Based Modeling in Economics
*W. Brian Arthur, Doyne Farmer, Tesfatsion, Bookstaber, Kirman*
Out-of-equilibrium economics, heterogeneous agents, increasing returns, and the Santa Fe Institute approach to markets as ecologies. Directly complements v5's Economics and Complex Systems tracks and makes the implicit "markets as complex adaptive systems" move explicit through simulation and model-building.

**Connects to:** Economics, Complex Systems, Game Theory, Multi-Agent Simulation, Artificial Life, Network Science

### Hypertext & Hypermedia
*Vannevar Bush, Ted Nelson, Doug Engelbart, Andy van Dam, Ward Cunningham*
Memex, Xanadu, NLS, HyperCard, wikis, Zettelkasten, and the lineage of linked-knowledge systems. Often folded into "History of Computing" but rich enough to stand alone, and particularly meaningful for an Obsidian vault — it *is* this topic's living realization.

**Connects to:** History of Computing, HCI, Information Retrieval, Writing Systems, Distributed Cognition, Memory & Mnemonics

### Information Architecture
*Wurman, Morville, Rosenfeld, Arango*
How knowledge gets structured for discovery — taxonomies, facets, wayfinding, findability, and the "polar bear" classification of IA foundations. The design-practice counterpart to the semantic-web / knowledge-graph track and directly applicable to maintaining a vault of this kind.

**Connects to:** Information Retrieval, HCI, Knowledge Graphs, Writing Systems, Cartography, Semiotics

### Philosophy of Time
*McTaggart, Bergson, Dainton, Rovelli, Le Poidevin*
A-series vs. B-series, tense, presentism vs. eternalism, the "specious present," duration, and Rovelli's recent thermodynamic account of time. A philosophical capstone to Thermodynamics, Relativity, and Phenomenology — the three existing vault topics in which the nature of time is already implicitly at play.

**Connects to:** Thermodynamics, Relativity, Phenomenology, Process Philosophy, Eastern Philosophy, Cognitive Science

### Cliodynamics & Mathematical History
*Turchin, Korotayev, Goldstone, Scheidel*
Quantitative history — secular cycles, elite overproduction, structural-demographic theory, and long-wave models of state collapse and recovery. A rare genuinely new field that applies complex systems and dynamical modeling to centuries-long historical data. A striking demonstration that the vault's favorite tools work on the human scale, not just the biological or computational.

**Connects to:** Complex Systems, Systems Thinking, Economics, Anthropology, History & Philosophy of Science, Dynamical Systems

### Chronobiology & Circadian Rhythms
*Pittendrigh, Halberg, Takahashi, Young, Foster*
Biological oscillators, entrainment, phase relationships, and the genetic-molecular basis of daily and seasonal rhythms. A concrete biological case of dynamical systems and a surprising bridge to music theory (rhythm, entrainment), cognitive science (attention, sleep), and architecture (lighting design).

**Connects to:** Dynamical Systems, Neuroscience, Evolution, Music Theory, Cognitive Science, Architecture

## Tier C — Interesting Niche Additions

Twelve topics that are narrower in scope but each has a distinctive contribution not offered by anything else in the vault. Each bridges two to three existing topics.

### Origami & Computational Folding
*Robert Lang, Erik Demaine, Tomohiro Tachi, David Huffman*
Mathematical origami, rigid folding, Miura-ori tessellations, self-folding materials, and the algorithms that turn a target shape into a crease pattern. A clean intersection of geometry, materials, and art with direct applications to deployable structures and metamaterials.

**Connects to:** Geometry & Topology, Materials Science, Biomimicry, Algorithmic Art, Architecture

### Islamic Geometric Patterns & Ornament
*Bourgoin, Critchlow, El-Said, Lu & Steinhardt*
Quasiperiodic tilings, girih patterns, the Topkapı Scroll, and the discovery that 15th-century craftsmen anticipated Penrose tilings by five centuries. A concrete bridge between sacred geometry, tiling mathematics, ornament theory, and architecture.

**Connects to:** Geometry & Topology, Architecture, Algorithmic Art, Typography, Pattern Languages

### Dance Notation & Movement Analysis
*Rudolf Laban, Benesh, Eshkol-Wachman, Ann Hutchinson Guest*
Labanotation, Benesh Movement Notation, effort-shape theory. The rare notational system for the moving body — a clean case study for the thesis that "writing systems shape what can be expressed," applied to a non-textual, non-auditory medium.

**Connects to:** Writing Systems & Notation, Semiotics, Phenomenology, Filmmaking, Music Theory (rhythm)

### Sound Design & Foley
*Walter Murch, Ben Burtt, Randy Thom, Jack Foley, Gary Rydstrom*
The designed soundscape of film and games, distinct from musical scoring. Murch's *In the Blink of an Eye* bridges sound design and editing; Ben Burtt's work on *Star Wars* is applied psychoacoustics. A missing companion to the vault's Filmmaking and Music Theory topics.

**Connects to:** Filmmaking, Music Theory, Musical Acoustics & Psychoacoustics, Software Synthesizer, Game Design

### Attention Studies & The Attention Economy
*Herbert Simon, James Williams, Yves Citton, Jonathan Crary, Tim Wu*
Attention as a scarce cognitive resource, the economics of its capture, and the political and ethical stakes. Extends cognitive science into media theory and HCI with a specifically contemporary edge.

**Connects to:** Cognitive Science, Media Theory, HCI, Economics, Philosophy of Mind

### Somatic Practices & Movement Science
*F.M. Alexander, Moshé Feldenkrais, Ida Rolf, Thomas Hanna*
Body-centered disciplines that treat movement as a cognitive practice. A practical complement to phenomenology and embodied cognition, and an often-overlooked branch of the "mind-body" track in both Western and Eastern traditions.

**Connects to:** Phenomenology, Cognitive Science, Ecological Psychology, Architecture (body in space)

### Comparative Mythology & Depth Psychology
*Jung, Campbell, Eliade, Hillman, Dumézil*
Archetypes, the monomyth, and structural patterns in myth across cultures. A psychological / anthropological counterpart to the more formal Rhetoric & Narrative topic, and a bridge to Jungian psychology's ongoing influence on design, film, and AI narrative generation.

**Connects to:** Rhetoric & Narrative, Anthropology, Semiotics, Filmmaking, Aesthetics

### Formal Verification & Model Checking
*Dijkstra, Hoare, Clarke, Emerson, Leslie Lamport*
Hoare logic, separation logic, TLA⁺, CTL / LTL, and how we prove that programs meet their specifications. A practical, industrial-grade application of logic and type theory with direct relevance to distributed systems.

**Connects to:** Logic & Formal Semantics, Type Theory, Distributed Systems (DDIA), Theory of Computation, Compiler Design

### Concurrency Theory
*Hoare (CSP), Milner (CCS, π-calculus), Hewitt (Actors), Lamport*
The mathematics and practice of concurrent computation. Deeper than the engineering-level treatment in Microservices or DDIA; the formal side of what those books handle as pragmatics.

**Connects to:** Distributed Systems (DDIA), Microservices, Theory of Computation, Logic, Type Theory

### History of Mathematics
*Morris Kline, Boyer, Struik, Grattan-Guinness*
The actual narrative of how mathematical ideas emerged — Babylonian tablets, Alexandrian geometry, Islamic algebra, European analysis, 19th-century rigor, 20th-century foundations. Complements Philosophy of Mathematics with concrete story and provides the historical grounding that makes category theory, topology, and information theory feel like events rather than eternal truths.

**Connects to:** Philosophy of Mathematics, Category Theory, Geometry, Greek Philosophy, History & Philosophy of Science

### Evolutionary Epistemology
*Donald T. Campbell, Popper, Lorenz, Ruse, Plotkin*
Applies Darwinian selection to the growth of knowledge itself — conjectures and refutations as selection pressures, cognitive architectures as evolved. The cleanest bridge between Evolution and Epistemology and a natural companion to v5's planned Philosophy of Science topic.

**Connects to:** Evolution, Epistemology, Philosophy of Science, Cognitive Science, AI

### Reservoir Computing & Neuromorphic Computation
*Jaeger, Maass, Mead, Indiveri, Tanaka*
Dynamical systems as computational substrate — echo state networks, liquid state machines, memristive hardware. A concrete fusion of dynamical systems, neuroscience, and computation that points beyond von Neumann architectures.

**Connects to:** Dynamical Systems, Neuroscience, Machine Learning, Theory of Computation, Artificial Life

## Editorial Notes

- **v6 is additive, not replacing.** v5 remains the canonical 56-topic roadmap; v6 supplies 35 rigorously filtered additions that respect v5's curation principle.
- **Tier A are the must-haves.** If only a handful of these were promoted, the eight Tier A topics would add the most cross-domain leverage to the existing vault, each connecting to five or more current topics.
- **No topic overlaps with an existing Knowledge folder** (verified against the 38 folders listed in `Knowledge/index.md` as of 2026-04-16).
